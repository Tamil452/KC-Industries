01

Built for automotive

A Tier-2 partner for OEM and Tier-1 programmes.

We were established in 1983 as an auto component manufacturer. Today we supply light and heavy vehicle OEMs and their Tier-1 suppliers, and we specialise in ball studs and ball pin rivets. Our CEO brings over three decades of experience in auto parts manufacturing, backed by a team of qualified young engineers.
