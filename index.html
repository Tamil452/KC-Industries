01 — Company profile

Four decades of turning, milling & fabrication.

Our manufacturing unit was established in 1983 as an auto component manufacturer and fabrication works. Today we supply original equipment manufacturers of light vehicles, heavy vehicles and the aviation field, and we specialise in Ball Stud and Ball Pin Rivet components.

Our CEO brings over three decades of experience in auto parts manufacturing, backed by a team of qualified, young engineers. Efficient use of resources lets us deliver quality parts with real cost reduction compared to the market.

VISION

Supply quality products that meet customer requirements, never miss on-time delivery, and keep improving continually.

AIM

Long-term, stable client relationships built on real solutions, with guarantee, reliability and maximum customer satisfaction.
