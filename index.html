About K.C. Industries

A partner for OEM, Tier 1 and Tier 2 programmes.

We are pioneers in machined components with Traub machines, started in 1983, and continue to serve industry by upgrading to the latest machinery in production and quality, offering customers the best prices within committed time schedules.

We are an ISO 9001:2015, IATF 16949:2016 & ZED-Silver certified company. Our team includes certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt employees, and we deploy the 7 QC tools and 5 core quality tools (APQP, FMEA, PPAP, SPC and MSA) to achieve the highest customer satisfaction, with a quality target of 0 PPM.

What we make

  • 01Precision machined components, especially Ball Stud, Ball Pin Rivets & Hydraulic Fitting
  • 02Sheet metal components, dies, fixtures and fabrication
  • 03Hydraulic cylinders and hydraulic power press machines

Why KCI

Since 1983 we have supplied OEM, Tier 1 and Tier 2 customers across automobile, aerospace, electrical & electronics and defence, and we specialise in ball studs and ball pin rivets.

  • Turning, milling, forming, grinding and thread rolling for tighter control of quality and lead time
  • Raw material – ferrous and non-ferrous
  • Heat treatment and surface treatment
  • 40+ machines, including 5-axis Swiss sliding head, CNC lathes and milling
  • Bar feeder and automation systems, Traub auto lathes for high-volume turned and stamping parts
  • Capacity for volume at competitive prices

Process flow

From bar stock to inspected part.

A typical route through our shop. Each part uses only the operations its drawing calls for.

  1. INBar stockRaw material in
  2. 01TurningCNC, Swiss-type & auto lathes
  3. 02MillingFlats, slots & cross features
  4. 03FormingShaping & profiles
  5. 04Thread rollingRolled threads
  6. 05GrindingCentreless grinding
  7. 06Heat treatmentTo the specified hardness
  8. 07Surface treatmentFinish & protection
  9. OUTInspected partChecked with instruments & gauges

Supporting operations: drilling, tapping, barrelling, slitting, and sheet-metal fabrication with welding for brackets.
